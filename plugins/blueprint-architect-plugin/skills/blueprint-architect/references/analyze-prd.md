# PRD analysis reference

Extract evidence before proposing architecture:

1. Identify primary users, their current problem, and the outcome they need.
2. Separate functional requirements from quality constraints such as latency, privacy, accessibility, compliance, scale, budget, and team capability.
3. Record explicit constraints and non-goals verbatim when precision matters.
4. Mark missing information as unknown; do not convert it into a requirement.
5. Map requirements to only the decisions that materially change cost, risk, data ownership, delivery speed, or future flexibility.
6. Treat a technology already mandated by the PRD as a constraint, unless it conflicts with another confirmed requirement.

Explain the fundamental product problem before producing the decision list. Recommendations must cite the requirement or constraint that makes them suitable. Do not invent timelines, traffic, budgets, or performance targets.
